# STEWARD: Purview retainer service catalogue (example: 16 h/month)

| Service | Included | Frequency | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health check (DLP, labels, retention, audit, eDiscovery) | ✅ | Monthly | 1-page status + KPI pack |
| DLP alert triage (true/false positive tuning) | ✅ up to 6 h | Weekly | Tuned policies, tuning log |
| Label & policy change requests | ✅ up to 4 changes | On request | Change record + test evidence |
| eDiscovery case support (holds, searches, exports) | ✅ up to 2 cases | On request | Case notes |
| Microsoft roadmap & Message Center review | ✅ | Monthly | "What changes for us" note |
| Copilot / AI data security (DSPM for AI review) | ✅ | Monthly | Findings + actions |
| Quarterly business review | ✅ | Quarterly | Slides: KPIs, risks, roadmap |
| Projects (new workloads, endpoint DLP rollout) | ❌ (separate SOW) | – | – |

**Response targets:** P1 (data leak in progress) same business day · P2 next business day · P3 within 5 days.
**Access:** least privilege via Purview role groups (no Global Admin); PIM for elevation; all changes logged.
