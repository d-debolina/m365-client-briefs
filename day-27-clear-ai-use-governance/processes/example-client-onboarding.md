## Purpose
Every new client is checked, set up and welcomed the same way, within 5 working days of signing.

## When it starts / ends
Starts when the engagement letter is signed. Ends when the welcome pack is sent and the client folder is live.

## Steps
| # | Who | Does what | Tool / template | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engagement partner | Confirms signed engagement letter is saved | Clients site › Engagements | Signed PDF |
| 2 | Compliance officer | Identity and conflict checks | Conflicts register | Check record |
| 3 | Operations | Creates client folder from template (restricted to the engagement team) | Clients site › *New client* template | Folder with correct permissions |
| 4 | Engagement manager | Writes welcome letter (Copilot draft allowed) | Word template `Welcome-letter.dotx` | Draft letter |
| 5 | Engagement partner | Reviews and signs off the letter – *human check* | File property *Reviewed by* | Approved letter |
| 6 | Operations | Sends welcome pack, sets first review date | Outlook + Planner | Sent pack, task |

## Where two old versions disagreed – and how we decided
| Topic | Version A | Version B | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who creates the folder | Engagement manager | Operations | Operations | Matches how it's actually done (observed 3 onboardings) |
| Conflict check timing | After kick-off | Before kick-off | Before | Regulatory requirement – compliance officer confirmed |
| Welcome letter | Partner writes | Manager drafts, partner signs | Manager drafts, partner signs | Fastest, keeps the partner accountable |

Rule of thumb: **what the regulator or insurer requires** → **what people actually do (observed)** → **what the owner decides**.

## AI use in this process
- Allowed: Copilot drafts the welcome letter from the engagement letter.
- Not allowed: AI tools outside Microsoft 365 for any step; AI for conflict checks.
- Human check: engagement partner, recorded in *Reviewed by*.
