# AI use policy – Fabrikam Advisory (fictional template)

**Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Operations Director · **Approved by:** Partners' meeting · **Next review:** 6 months after approval

> Plain English on purpose. If a sentence needs a lawyer to explain it, rewrite it.

## 1. Why we have this policy
We use AI tools to save time. Our clients, our insurer and the law expect us to protect their information
and to stand behind every piece of work we send. This policy explains how to do both.

## 2. Which tools you may use
| Tool | Status | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 Copilot (work account) | ✅ Approved | Drafting, summarising, searching our own files |
| Copilot Chat (work account, web grounding) | ✅ Approved | General research, no client names |
| ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude on a **personal** account | ⛔ Not allowed for work | Nothing that contains firm or client information |
| Any other AI tool | ❓ Ask first | Send a request to the IT lead; we decide within 5 working days |

The current list lives in the **AI tools register** (`discovery/ai-tools-register.csv`). If a tool isn't on it, it isn't approved.

## 3. What you must never put into an AI tool
- Client information in any tool that isn't on the approved list
- HR records, health information, salaries, passwords or bank details – in **any** AI tool
- Documents marked **Confidential – Client** unless the tool is approved for that label

## 4. Checking AI-assisted work (the "human sign-off" rule)
1. **You own it.** If you used AI, you are responsible for every word, number and reference.
2. **Check facts and figures** against the source document, not against the AI's summary.
3. **Client deliverables need a second pair of eyes.** A reviewer at Manager level or above reads it before it goes out.
4. **Record it.** Tick *AI-assisted: yes* and the reviewer's name in the deliverable's properties (or the file note).
5. **When in doubt, don't send it.** Ask your reviewer.

## 5. Telling clients
If a client asks, we tell them truthfully how AI was used and how we checked the work. A one-paragraph
statement for proposals and insurer questionnaires is in Appendix A.

## 6. Keeping our files Copilot-ready
- Save final versions in the team site, not in personal OneDrive or email.
- Mark old versions **Superseded** or move them to the Archive library.
- If you can open a folder you shouldn't, tell the IT lead. Don't "just not look".

## 7. Breaches and questions
Report a mistake to the IT lead the same day. Honest mistakes reported quickly are a training issue, not a disciplinary one.

---
### Appendix A – statement for clients and insurers
*"Fabrikam Advisory uses Microsoft 365 Copilot under our business licence. Client information is only processed
in approved tools inside our Microsoft 365 tenant. Every client deliverable that used AI assistance is checked
and signed off by a qualified reviewer, and we keep a record of that review."*
