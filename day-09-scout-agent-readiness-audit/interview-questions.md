# Process interview questions (30 min per process)
1. Walk me through the last time you did this, step by step.
2. Which systems did you open? What did you copy between them?
3. Where do you wait for someone else?
4. What goes wrong most often?
5. How many times a week/month does this happen? How long each time?
6. Which decisions need a human, by policy or by judgement?
7. What would "done well" look like in six months?
