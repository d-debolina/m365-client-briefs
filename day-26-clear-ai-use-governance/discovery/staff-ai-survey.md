Send as an anonymous Microsoft Forms survey. Say clearly: *"Nobody gets in trouble for answering honestly.
We want to approve the tools you find useful, not take them away."*

1. Which AI tools have you used for work in the last month? (tick all)
   Microsoft 365 Copilot · Copilot Chat · ChatGPT · Gemini · Claude · Perplexity · Grammarly AI · Otter / meeting note-takers · Other (text)
2. Which account did you use? Work account · Personal free account · Personal paid account · Not sure
3. What do you use it for? (tick all) Drafting emails · Summarising documents · Research · Client deliverables · Data / spreadsheets · Meeting notes · Other
4. Have you ever pasted client or HR information into a non-Copilot tool? Yes · No · Not sure
5. Has Copilot ever shown you an out-of-date document or a file you didn't expect to see? Yes (please describe, no names) · No
6. Which tool would you most like the firm to approve, and why? (text)
7. Your team: Advisory · Audit · Tax · Operations · Partners (keep groups ≥ 5 people so answers stay anonymous)

Combine the answers with the technical signals (`ai-tools-register.csv`) – neither is complete on its own.
